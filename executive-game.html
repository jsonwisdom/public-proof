Executive-layer protocol

1 · SOURCE
2 · AUTHORITY
3 · DELEGATION
4 · IMPLEMENTATION
5 · REVIEW
6 · REPLAY

EXEC_SOURCE

Capture exact title, source URL, action type, publication page, and quoted operative text when needed.

EXEC_AUTHORITY

Separate cited constitutional/statutory authority from the policy result being asserted.

EXEC_DELEGATION

Identify who is directed, what is delegated, what remains presidential, and what requires another actor.

EXEC_IMPLEMENTATION

Require agency rules, contracts, guidance, payments, enforcement actions, or other implementation receipts before claiming execution.

EXEC_REVIEW

Keep congressional oversight, inspector-general work, litigation, and court orders on separate rails.

EXEC_REPLAY

Run the same object forward and backward. A later headline does not rewrite the original source object.

WHITEHOUSE_GOV_POST != STATUTE
EXECUTIVE_ORDER != COURT_JUDGMENT
PRESIDENTIAL_ASSERTION != IMPLEMENTATION_RECEIPT
DELEGATION != PERSONAL_EXECUTION
INVESTIGATION != FINDING
MISSING_RECEIPT != PROOF_OF_NONOCCURRENCE
MEDIA_SUMMARY != PRIMARY_SOURCE
